## Use of the name without registration

Motivus (a US consulting company) presents an "AnonyMed" accelerator for de-identifying clinical data and generating synthetic health data (motivus.com/accelerators-and-frameworks/anonymed/, page live on 30 October 2026, no ™ or ® shown). No trade mark application was found for it. In Germany an unregistered sign is protected only with market recognition (section 4 no. 2 MarkenG), which a product page of a US consultancy does not establish; in the US, common-law rights would be limited to actual use there.

## Assessment

For a free, open-source tool published from Germany the name is usable. The remaining risks are a later application by Motivus and confusion with their product in search results. If the project is ever offered commercially, file a word mark (DE or EU, classes 9, 42, 44) first and reconsider the .com situation.
