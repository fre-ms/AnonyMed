## Assessment

No identical trade mark, application, product or company name was found, and no web page uses the name. All the nearer marks share the prefix "pseudo" or "pseudonym", which describes what the software does and therefore weighs little in a comparison. They differ from "PseudonyMed" in their endings (-i, -Mate, -io, -ex, -omia, -izer). The closest in spelling and sound is PSEUDONYMI (France, class 9, software): it shares the first nine letters and differs only in the ending. A conflict there is unlikely for a free, open-source tool, but it cannot be ruled out if the tool is ever offered commercially in France. The German applications PseudoMate and Pseudonio cover classes 9 and 42, but their lists of goods and services have not been published yet.

There is a second weakness, in the name itself. "Pseudonym" plus "Med" may be treated as descriptive or non-distinctive for software that pseudonymises medical documents (section 8(2) nos. 1 and 2 MarkenG; Article 7(1)(b) and (c) EUTMR). A word mark might therefore meet objections, and rights against similar names would be narrow. The offices did register Pseudonymizer and PSEUDOMIZER, so refusal is not certain. A figurative mark, or a name with a distinctive element, would be easier to protect. AnonyMed has the same structure.

The domain position is good: .de, .com, .net, .eu, .app and .io are free, and .org is presumably the project's own.

Remaining risks: TMview is not an official register; very recent filings, and filings claiming a foreign priority of up to six months, may not be visible yet; company names and unregistered signs (section 5 MarkenG, section 4 no. 2 MarkenG) can only be searched for partially; and several "pseudo" applications were filed in 2026 alone in classes 9 and 42, so more are likely to follow.

Before any commercial offer:

1. Have a professional clearance search done, including company names and use in the target markets.
2. Look at the published lists of goods and services for PseudoMate and Pseudonio once they appear.
3. File a German or EU word mark (or word-and-figurative mark) in classes 9, 42 and 44 before launch, and add France or an international registration if the tool is to be offered there.
4. Register pseudonymed.de and, if wanted, .com and .eu.
5. Set up monitoring for new "pseudo" filings.
